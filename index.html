Origin of ORPHEUS

Long before the public channels appeared, ORPHEUS existed as a recovery engine built to catalogue data that should have vanished. The engine began flagging one symbol over and over: the eye. Operators called it a bug. The archive called it a witness.

The Fracture Event

During the Fracture Event, seven sealed messages escaped into unrelated servers, game descriptions, image metadata, and broken page titles. Anyone who stitched the fragments together discovered the first promotion phrase and vanished from the public roster.
